# Users and visual system

## Primary users

**Rider / demo operator.** Select a rider, see their wallet, book a vehicle,
understand why a booking failed, follow the trip through its statuses, and
verify the resulting debit without knowing SQL.

**Fleet operations manager.** Browse a large fleet, inspect individual
vehicles, find nearby available rides, monitor revenue trends, refresh lifetime
totals, and understand review themes by city.

## Visual system

The interface uses a deep forest navigation surface, warm paper background,
lime action accent and coral warning accent. Manrope keeps dense operational
tables readable; DM Mono distinguishes IDs, timestamps and money. Panels,
buttons, badges, form controls, loading states and empty/error messages are
reused across all screens. The sidebar collapses on phone-sized screens, tables
scroll horizontally, and analytical grids collapse to one column.
